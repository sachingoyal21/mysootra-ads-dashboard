The rules behind the calls
  • Budget is — for the whole of November.
  • Volume ads aim for — per profile visit and stop above —.
  • Quality ads aim for — and stop above —. An ad counts as Quality only when its ad name or ad set name contains the word Quality.
  • An ad gets a call after 2 days with spend and — spent or 150 profile visits. Before that it shows Wait.
  • Stop also fires when cost rises 3 days in a row above target, when fewer than 1 in 100 viewers visit the profile, or when the same people keep seeing the ad and fewer of them visit.
  • Scale needs cost at or under target, a steady last day, the ad seen under 2 times per person in 7 days, and the daily budget used in full. The step is 20% at a time, with 2 to 3 days between raises.
  • One ad gets at most 40% of the live daily budgets, which is — a day right now. Keep — a day free for testing new posts.
  • A raise is paid for by lowering the weakest Hold ad, or by the budget of a stopped ad. If cost is 25% or more worse for 2 days after a raise, go back to the previous budget.
  • Data is pulled from Meta every 3 hours and saved on our own server, so the history stays even if Meta drops it.
