How the calls are made
  • An ad gets a call after 2 days with spend and ₹300 spent or 150 profile visits. Before that it shows Wait.
  • Stop needs the 3-day cost per visit above the stop line on at least 2 of the last 3 days. One bad day is not enough.
  • An ad that brings followers cheaper than the account average is kept on Hold, even if its visits are costly.
  • A cost that rises 3 days in a row is a warning on Hold, not a Stop.
  • Scale needs cost per visit at or under target, a steady last day, the budget used in full, and followers that are not far costlier than average.
  • Budgets move 20% at a time. Money from a stopped ad goes to a new post test.
  • Stop still fires at once for an ad that is far over the limit, shown too often to the same people, or visited by fewer than 1 in 100 viewers.
