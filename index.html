    How the calls are made
    • The goal is followers, so an ad is judged on cost per follower first. This needs 30 follows credited to the ad in the last 3 finished days. Until then the visit rules below apply and the ad cannot be raised.
    • An ad gets a call after 2 days with spend and ₹300 spent, 150 profile visits or 100 follows. Before that it shows Wait.
    • Scale needs all of these. Followers at or under ₹1.20 each, yesterday not more than 15% costlier, the ad seen under 2 times per person in 7 days, the budget used in full, and no raise in the last 2 days. The ₹1.20 comes from the followers still needed and the budget left.
    • When a profile visit costs more than a follower, people are following straight from the ad. The card calls this out as a strong sign.
    • Only one ad is raised a day, the one with the cheapest followers. A raise is 50% while the ad set is under ₹500 a day, then 20%. Go back a step if cost is 25% worse for 2 days. One ad gets at most 40% of the live daily budgets.
    • Stop needs all of these. Followers above ₹1.80 each over 3 full days, ₹300 spent since follows were reported, and yesterday also above ₹1.80. An ad that did well over its whole run gets Cut budget first.
    • Day-3 check. From its 3rd day an ad is a Stop if it has under 100 profile visits and under 30 follows, or if its followers cost more than ₹2.00 each. One exception. If its visits cost under ₹1.00, followers between ₹2.00 and ₹2.50 are a Hold with no raise, and ₹2.50 or more is a Stop. If it has too few follows to judge, a visit cost above ₹2.00 is a Stop. An ad with cheap followers is kept even when its visits cost more than ₹2.00.
    • Cheap followers with costly visits. When followers cost under ₹1.50 but a profile visit costs ₹2.50 or more, the ad is on Hold with no raise, as long as follower gains keep up. Keeping up means yesterday’s follows were at least 80% of its daily average before that. If gains slow, it is a Stop.
    • Between ₹1.20 and ₹1.80 is Hold. Every card shows the ad’s rank on cost per follower.
    • If every ad gets costlier on the same day, nothing is stopped. Check the account first.
    • Visit rules, used only when an ad has too few follows. Under 1.5% of viewers visiting and over ₹1.50 a visit is a Stop. With 2% or more it gets up to 10 days on Hold. A proven ad whose visit cost doubles gets Cut budget first.
    • Give every festival ad an end date on the festival day when you set it up. The dashboard cannot see festival dates.
    • A new ad starts at ₹150 a day. Money from a stopped ad goes to the day’s new ad.
    • Stop still fires at once for an ad shown too often to the same people, or far over the visit limit with no cheap followers to show for it.
